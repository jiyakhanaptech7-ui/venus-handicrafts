About Venus Handicrafts

Since 1972, Venus Handicrafts has been a trusted name in manufacturing and exporting premium decorative handicrafts to global importers, wholesalers, and retailers. Our dedication to quality and artistry has earned us recognition on the global platform.

We specialize in creating exquisite pieces that reflect the rich cultural heritage of craftsmanship while incorporating modern design elements. Each item is meticulously crafted by skilled artisans using traditional techniques passed down through generations.

Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has helped us maintain our market value and reputation worldwide. We continuously evolve our designs to meet contemporary tastes while preserving the essence of authentic handicraft traditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, ordering, and shipping.

What materials are used in your handicrafts?
We use a variety of high-quality materials including iron, brass, wood, glass, aluminium, and natural fibers. All materials are carefully selected for durability, aesthetics, and sustainability.
How long does shipping take?
Domestic shipping within United States typically takes 5-7 business days. International shipping times vary by destination, usually between 20-30 business days. Expedited shipping options are available.
Do you offer custom design services?
Yes, we offer custom design services for bulk orders. Our artisans can work with you to create unique pieces tailored to your specific requirements. Contact our sales team for more information.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for items in original condition. Custom-made items are non-returnable unless damaged or defective. Please contact our customer service to initiate a return.
How do I care for my handicraft items?
Care instructions vary by material. Generally, dust regularly with a soft cloth. Avoid direct sunlight, moisture, and harsh chemicals. Specific care instructions are provided with each product.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes, we offer competitive wholesale pricing for retailers, interior designers, and corporate clients. Please contact our wholesale department for pricing and minimum order requirements.
